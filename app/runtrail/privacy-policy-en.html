RUNTRAIL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Your workout records and videos are primarily stored on your devices. You control health, location, and recording permissions and choose when to share content with others.

1. Scope

This policy explains how RunTrail (“we”) handles personal information in its iPhone and Apple Watch apps, including run tracking, workout metrics, routes, video recording, device communication, and Pro subscriptions. RunTrail does not require an app account and does not operate its own server to receive workout records or videos.

2. Information and purposes

  • Health and fitness: With your authorization, Apple HealthKit, Motion & Fitness, and device sensors provide run dates, duration, distance, speed, pace, heart rate, steps, cadence, stride length, vertical oscillation, altitude, elevation gain, splits, and workout summaries. These support live displays, calculations, saved runs, and video overlays. Available metrics depend on your device, workout mode, and permissions. Health information is sensitive personal information.
  • Precise location and routes: Coordinates, location timestamps, accuracy, and altitude support outdoor distance measurement, route recording, maps, and route overlays in videos. Precise location and movement history are sensitive information; refusing access affects these features.
  • Video and audio: The camera and microphone capture running videos and sound. Selected workout metrics, routes, and styles are composited into videos on your device. Videos, thumbnails, and clip details are stored locally. Overlays become part of the video image.
  • Preferences and subscriptions: We store display layouts, workout settings, and subscription entitlement status. Apple StoreKit provides product, transaction verification, expiration, and renewal information to support purchases, restoration, and Pro access. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • Contacting us: If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide, to respond to and handle your request.

3. Permissions and choices

Relevant features request Health read/write, location, Motion & Fitness, camera, microphone, or add-only Photos permission. Health write access saves running workouts, distance, and routes to Apple Health. Photos access adds recorded videos and does not browse your entire photo library. During a workout, Apple Watch may continue processing workout and route data in the background as permitted by the system.

You can change or revoke permissions in system settings and Apple Health’s app permissions. Refusing or revoking access may disable related features or result in incomplete data; it does not delete previously saved information. Sensitive information is used only for the relevant features you enable and authorize.

4. Storage, device communication, and Apple services

Workout records, routes, videos, and preferences are primarily stored on your devices. Your iPhone and paired Apple Watch use Apple communication mechanisms to transfer workout metrics, summaries, routes, settings, and entitlement status for cross-device functionality.

Maps and map overlays use Apple MapKit, which may request map content for the relevant area from Apple. Purchasing and restoring subscriptions use Apple’s App Store services. Apple’s policies govern data processing by these system services. Depending on your system settings, device backups, Apple Health, or Photos may be backed up or synced through Apple’s iCloud services. This is separate from any RunTrail-operated cloud service, which we do not provide.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy and Health App & Privacy for information about Apple’s processing.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information, integrate third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, or use health or location data for advertising, marketing profiles, or data trading. The app does not automatically upload your workout records or videos to us.

When you save to Photos or use system sharing, the content is provided to the service or recipient you choose, whose privacy policy governs subsequent handling. Videos may visibly contain health metrics and routes. Review their contents before sending. Information you voluntarily send us is used only to address your request; any legally required disclosure will be limited to what is necessary.

6. Retention and deletion

Local workout records and videos remain available until you delete them or remove the app’s data from the relevant device. You can delete local runs or clips in workout history; deleting a run also removes its associated local videos.

Deleting app records does not automatically delete copies in Apple Health, Photos, another device, system backups, or a recipient’s possession. Manage these separately in the relevant app, device, or service. Photos’ Recently Deleted album and system backups may retain copies. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions through Apple.

Information you email us is retained only as needed to resolve your request and necessary follow-up, unless retention is required by law. We cannot directly access or delete information stored solely on your devices.

7. Security and privacy rights

We rely on system permissions, app sandboxing, and device protections to limit access. Protect your device credentials and take care when sharing files containing health or route information. No method of storage or transmission guarantees absolute security.

You can view and delete local records, save or share videos, and revoke system permissions. Contact us below to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or other applicable privacy rights. We will handle requests according to their nature and applicable requirements, and may verify your identity where necessary.

8. Minors

RunTrail is not specifically directed at children. Minors should use health, location, recording, and sharing features with guidance from a parent or guardian and obtain guardian consent where required by applicable law. Contact us if you believe a minor’s information has been improperly provided to us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and its effective date when features or data practices change. We will communicate material changes through the app or another appropriate channel and obtain renewed consent before the relevant processing when required by law.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or personal information requests, contact RunTrail at twl7@foxmail.com.
